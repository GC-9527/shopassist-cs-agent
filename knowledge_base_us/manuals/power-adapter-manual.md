# USB-C Charger & Power Bank Manual

## Before first use
Check that the plug type matches your outlet and that the cable and connector are undamaged. The charger accepts 100-240V input, so a plug adapter is enough for travel; a voltage converter is not needed.

## Charging speed
Actual charging speed depends on the device's supported protocol. A charger rated above the device's requirement is safe, but it will not charge faster than the device negotiates. Using a cable rated below the charger's output limits speed.

## Temperature
The charger may become warm during fast charging. It should not become too hot to hold. Keep it uncovered and away from bedding while charging.

## Safety
Stop using the product immediately if it swells, leaks, emits odour, or shows burn marks. Keep it away from flammable materials and contact support.
