# Wireless Earbuds User Manual

## Pairing
Place both earbuds in the charging case for 10 seconds, then take them out. The indicator flashes white to show pairing mode. Select the model in your phone's Bluetooth list.

## One earbud silent
Remove the old pairing record on the phone, then press and hold both earbuds for 8 seconds until they reset and re-pair with each other.

## Water resistance
The earbuds are sweat resistant, not waterproof. The charging case is not water resistant at all. Dry the earbuds with a soft cloth and make sure the charging contacts are dry before putting them back.

## Cleaning
Wipe the acoustic mesh with a dry soft brush. Do not soak the mesh in alcohol or any liquid.

## Battery care
Charge the case with the supplied cable. Avoid leaving the case in a hot car. Battery swelling means the product must be taken out of service immediately.
