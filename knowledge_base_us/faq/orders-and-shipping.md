# Orders & Shipping FAQ

## Tracking not updating
Carriers sometimes take up to 24 hours to post the first scan after the label is created. If there is no tracking movement for 48 hours after pickup, or the parcel is past its estimated delivery date by more than 2 business days, we can open a carrier trace (`LOG-TRACE-48`).

## Marked delivered but not received
Check the delivery location, building access notes and any household members first. If the parcel still cannot be found, we open a "delivered not received" case with the carrier. We do not refund on the customer's word alone before the carrier investigation closes.

## Changing the address
Once an order has left the warehouse, the address cannot always be changed. We check the live carrier status first; if the carrier supports an intercept we escalate to a human agent. The assistant never edits a shipping address directly.

## Delivery estimates
Estimated delivery dates are calculated at checkout and are not guarantees. Weather, customs and carrier volume can extend them.
