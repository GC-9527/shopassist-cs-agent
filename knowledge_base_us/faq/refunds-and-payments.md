# Refunds & Payments FAQ

## How long refunds take
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually post in 3-5 business days after we issue them; PayPal is often faster. This is a general timing reference, not the status of a specific refund.

## Changing the refund destination
For security we cannot redirect a refund to a different card, bank account or PayPal address. If the original payment method is closed, the case must go to a human agent to arrange an alternative.

## Checking a specific refund
A specific refund status requires the order number and a lookup in the payment system. The assistant cannot estimate it from this page.

## Declined or reversed payments
If a payment was declined, the order is not created. If a charge was reversed by the issuer, the order is placed on hold and reviewed before shipping.
