# International Orders

## Duties and taxes
Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties and taxes charged by the destination country. These are the recipient's responsibility unless the checkout page states that duties are prepaid.

## Where orders ship from
In-stock items ship from the regional warehouse (US orders from the US warehouse, EU orders from the EU hub), typically 2-5 business days. Items out of stock regionally ship from the Shenzhen facility and take 7-14 business days, with tracking that may not update until the parcel clears import.

## Delivery times
International delivery typically takes 7-14 business days after dispatch. Customs clearance can add time and is outside our control.

## Restricted destinations
Some products containing lithium batteries cannot ship to certain destinations or must travel by ground. The checkout page blocks unsupported combinations.

## Right of withdrawal (EU/UK)
Customers in the EU and UK have a 14-day right of withdrawal from delivery, separate from our 30-day return policy. Sealed hygiene items are excluded once unsealed.
