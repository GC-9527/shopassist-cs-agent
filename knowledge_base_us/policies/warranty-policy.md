# Limited Warranty

## Coverage
Rule ID `WAR-18M`. Consumer products carry an 18-month limited warranty from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects in battery, charging circuitry, Bluetooth and speakers.

## Exclusions
Damage from liquid beyond the product's rated IP level, drops, crushing, unauthorised disassembly, or third-party repair is not covered. Normal battery capacity loss over time is not a defect.

## Claim process
A warranty claim requires the order number, the serial number if printed on the product, and a description or short video of the fault. We issue an RMA and either replace, repair, or refund after inspection. Do not send items back before an RMA is issued.

## Where service happens
Replacement units ship from the regional warehouse, so most warranty replacements arrive in 3-7 business days. Repairs where offered are handled by our regional service partners; the product is not shipped to the manufacturing site for a consumer warranty claim.
