# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Rule ID `RET-30D`. Customers may return most items within 30 calendar days of delivery. The window starts on the delivery date shown by the carrier, not the order date.

## Condition requirements
Items must include all original accessories, manuals and packaging. Normal inspection (opening the box, charging once, pairing to check function) does not void the return. Items must not show signs of prolonged use.

## Exclusions
Hygiene-sealed products (in-ear headphones) cannot be returned for change of mind once the hygiene seal is broken. Gift cards and digital licenses are non-returnable after activation. Customised items are non-returnable unless the wrong item was shipped.

## Return shipping
Defective or incorrectly shipped items: we provide a prepaid label. Change-of-mind returns: the customer pays return shipping, or we deduct the label cost from the refund.

## Where returns go
Returns are sent to our regional returns center in the destination country, not back to the manufacturing site. US customers return to our US warehouse; EU customers return to our EU hub. The return label generated by the tool always points to the correct regional address.

## Refund timing
Once the return arrives at the regional returns center and passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Bank posting time depends on the issuer and is outside our control.
