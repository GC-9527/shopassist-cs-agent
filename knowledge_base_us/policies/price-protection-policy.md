# Price Protection

## Eligibility
Rule ID `PRICE-30D`. If the same item at the same specification and seller drops in price within 30 days of the order date, the customer may request a price adjustment.

## Exclusions
Flash sales, limited-time doorbusters, bundle pricing, member-only coupons that cannot be reused, gift-value changes, and cross-seller comparisons are not eligible.

## Refund method
Approved adjustments are returned to the original payment method or as store credit, as stated on the activity page. Price protection is a payment action and must be shown to the customer with the amount and method before it is submitted.
