# Audio Troubleshooting

## Pairing fails or only one side connects
Reset both earbuds together (hold both for 8 seconds), then remove the old Bluetooth entry on the phone and pair again. Make sure the case is charged above 20%.

## Sound cuts out
Interference from Wi-Fi routers and USB 3.0 ports can affect 2.4 GHz Bluetooth. Move away from the source and test with a different device to separate a phone problem from an earbud problem.

## Microphone quality
Check that the correct earbud is worn in the correct ear, and that the microphone opening is not blocked by the ear tip or by debris.

## Static or distortion
Test at lower volume. Persistent distortion at normal volume indicates a hardware fault and is covered by the limited warranty.
