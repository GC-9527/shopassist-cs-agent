# Charging & Power Troubleshooting

## Device does not power on
Check the outlet, cable, adapter and any switch or removable battery in that order. Cross-test with a known-good adapter of the same specification. Do not use an adapter rated above the device's input voltage.

## Slow or intermittent charging
Try a different cable, adapter and outlet first. Check the port for lint or debris. Very cold or very hot environments reduce charging speed, and fast charging pauses when the device is under heavy load. Record the charger specification and ambient temperature if the problem persists.

## Safety stop
Swollen, punctured, leaking or unusually hot batteries must not be charged or used further. Move the product away from flammable material and contact support. Never advise a customer to puncture, compress or replace a built-in battery themselves.
