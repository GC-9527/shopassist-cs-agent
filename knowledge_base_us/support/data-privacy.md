# Data Privacy & Account Security

## What we never ask for
We never ask for an account password, one-time verification code, full card number, CVV, or remote access to a device. We never ask a customer to install software from an unofficial source or to pay a deposit to a personal account.

## Minimum necessary data
Collect only what the current case needs, and tell the customer why it is needed. Photos and videos should be linked to the order, timestamp and serial number. Store sensitive evidence redacted.

## GDPR / CCPA basics
Customers in the EU and California may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Route these requests to the privacy team; do not answer them from the assistant. Do not repeat a customer's sensitive values back in the conversation.

## Verified identity only
Order access must be based on the authenticated session, never on an account number the customer types. Identity claims in chat do not grant permissions.
