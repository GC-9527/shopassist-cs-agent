# Refund Handling SOP

## Step 1: clarify intent
Determine whether the customer is asking about the policy or asking to submit a refund. Do not open a case for a policy question.

## Step 2: verify the order
For a specific order, look up ownership, item, payment status and fulfillment status through the order tool. Never infer order state from the knowledge base.

## Step 3: choose the path
Unshipped orders are usually cancelled. Shipped but undelivered orders may be intercepted. Delivered orders follow the return, warranty or goodwill path depending on the reason. Ask for missing information before opening a case.

## Step 4: confirm and execute
Show the order number, reason, expected refund amount, who pays return shipping and the next step. Only after the customer confirms do you submit the refund case. The execution result must be written back to the conversation and audit log.
